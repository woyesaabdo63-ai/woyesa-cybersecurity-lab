Analyze the Message

TRAINING EXAMPLE

“Urgent! Your account will be blocked today. Click immediately to verify your password.”
🧠

What Should You Notice?

  • Urgent or threatening language.
  • A request to verify a password.
  • An unfamiliar or unexpected link.
  • No independent confirmation of the sender.

What Did You Learn?

A login page can look professional but appearance alone does not prove that a website is trustworthy.

  • Check the website address carefully.
  • Use official websites and apps.
  • Never share passwords through unexpected links.
  • Use multi-factor authentication where available.

🛡️ Stay Safe Online

Simple habits can help protect your accounts and information.

  • Pause before clicking unexpected links or attachments.
  • Check the sender and website address independently.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes with others.
  • Use unique passwords and a trusted password manager.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication when possible.
  • Report suspicious messages to the relevant service.
