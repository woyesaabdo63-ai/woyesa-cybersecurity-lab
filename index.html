4. Remember These Safety Tips

  • Check the sender's address carefully.
  • Be cautious of urgent threats and pressure.
  • Check website domains before opening unfamiliar links.
  • Never share passwords through suspicious links.
  • Visit the official website directly when in doubt.
  • Use multi-factor authentication when available.
