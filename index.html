STEP 4 — SECURITY LESSON

🛡️ Stay Safe Online

Remember these important cybersecurity habits.

1. Check the sender

Unexpected messages may pretend to come from trusted organizations. Verify the sender independently.

2. Think before clicking

Do not rush because a message threatens to close your account.

3. Protect your password

Never share your password or verification codes through unexpected links or messages.

4. Visit official websites

When uncertain, open the official app or type the known website address yourself.

5. Enable extra protection

Use multi-factor authentication when available.

Remember:

A professional-looking website is not automatically trustworthy.
